## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-06

### Added
- 🎉 Initial release of **ButanoDeath**.
- Plays the iconic death audio theme automatically when the player dies.
- In-game test commands: `/bd`, `/butano`, and `/butanodeath`.
- Custom high-resolution addon logo for GitHub, WowUp, and Wago.
- Dual documentation support: Spanish (`README.md`) and English (`README_EN.md`).
- Multi-interface version support in `.toc` (WoW Forever 1.60.x, Classic Era, and Retro clients).
